Platform Capabilities

Engineered for Technical Rigor & Data Integrity

1. Rigorous ML Forecasting Pipeline

Re-architected LSTM neural networks, ARIMA autoregression, Random Forest, and Linear Regression baselines. Strictly chronological train/test splits, scalers fit only on training data, and honest out-of-sample MAE, RMSE, and Directional Accuracy.

2. Mathematical Indicator Engine

Pure Python & Pandas computations of SMA (20, 50, 200), EMA (12, 26, 50), Wilder's smoothed RSI (14), MACD, Bollinger Bands (20, 2σ), and ATR with educational explanations and risk boundaries.

3. Accounting-Grade Portfolio Ledger

Persistent database transaction models. Accurate calculation of per-stock holdings, average acquisition costs, realized profit from sales, unrealized mark-to-market P&L, and strict validation against overselling.

4. Grounded AI Financial Assistant

Tool-grounded research assistant interfacing Google Gemini API or deterministic analytics engines. Answers stock questions using verified platform data rather than hallucinating price predictions.

5. Financial News Sentiment NLP

Financial media news extraction with VADER sentiment scoring. Evaluates positive, negative, and neutral market sentiment per equity with explicit disclaimers regarding causation.

6. Dual Currency: INR & USD

Proper handling of Indian NSE symbols in INR (₹) and US equities in USD ($). Does not mix raw numbers without explicit currency exchange rate translation.
